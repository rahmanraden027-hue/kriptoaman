# KAM Tokenomics v1

Status: **Adopted project economic baseline — pre-commercial launch / not yet verified on-chain**

KAM is the native asset of the KriptoAman network. This document establishes the approved project economic baseline for KAM. Adoption of this document does not by itself declare a public/commercial mainnet launch, exchange listing, guaranteed liquidity, regulatory approval, or guaranteed token price. Supply figures must be reconciled with final production genesis/on-chain state before being submitted externally as verified on-chain supply.

## Core parameters

- Asset: KriptoAman
- Symbol: KAM
- Role: Native asset of KriptoAman Mainnet
- Chain ID: 22028 (`0x560c`)
- Decimals: 18
- Maximum supply baseline: **1,000,000,000 KAM**
- Target initial circulating supply: **50,000,000 KAM (5%)**
- Guaranteed return: No
- Guaranteed price: No
- Automatic daily tokenomics changes: No
- Burn mechanism enabled in v1: No

The 50,000,000 KAM figure is a target for initial circulation, not a claim that this amount is currently circulating. Actual circulating supply must be calculated from unlocked, transferable balances and independently reconciled against public on-chain evidence before any tracked-market submission.

## Vesting and release framework

- **Team & Contributors:** 12-month cliff, then linear vesting over 36 months.
- **Strategic Partnerships:** documented time- or milestone-based vesting; an initial 6–12 month lock may be used where appropriate before staged release.
- **Ecosystem & Network Development:** milestone- and governance-controlled release.
- **Community & Adoption:** program- and milestone-controlled release.
- **Validator / Network Incentives:** released according to documented network-security and validator incentive rules; locked reserves are not circulating.
- **Treasury / Foundation:** governance-controlled release.
- **Liquidity & Market Development:** release only when launch-readiness and applicable compliance requirements are satisfied.

## Circulating-supply methodology

A KAM unit should be classified as circulating only when it is unlocked, transferable, and genuinely available to the public market under the published distribution policy. Locked team, treasury, validator, ecosystem, strategic, or otherwise restricted balances must not be counted merely because they exist at genesis or in an allocation wallet.

Before an external tracked listing submission, publish and reconcile:

1. canonical allocation wallet addresses;
2. lock/vesting mechanisms and schedules;
3. current unlocked balances;
4. a reproducible circulating-supply calculation with timestamp;
5. any issuance, validator reward, burn, or other supply-change mechanism actually implemented by the protocol.

Maximum supply must not be represented as technically enforced on-chain until the final production protocol/genesis configuration independently demonstrates that property.

## Utility direction

KAM is intended to support the KriptoAman ecosystem. Utility direction includes network transaction fees, application and developer ecosystem participation, infrastructure services, ecosystem incentives, and future governance functions where technically and legally appropriate.

No utility in this document should be interpreted as a promise of financial return, price appreciation, guaranteed listing, or guaranteed liquidity.

## US$29 indicative valuation scenario

US$29 per KAM is an **indicative valuation scenario reference only**. It is not the current market price, not a guaranteed listing price, and not an investment promise.

If an independent market were hypothetically to price KAM at US$29:

- 50,000,000 KAM target circulating supply × US$29 = **US$1.45 billion hypothetical circulating market capitalization**.
- 1,000,000,000 KAM maximum-supply baseline × US$29 = **US$29 billion hypothetical fully diluted valuation**.

Actual KAM price, market capitalization, volume and liquidity must come from genuine independently verifiable market activity. The liquidity allocation must not be used for wash trading, fabricated volume, artificial price support, or misleading market activity.

## Governance safeguards

1. Supply, allocation, vesting, treasury, liquidity and burn parameters must not be changed automatically.
2. Material economic changes require explicit approval, versioned documentation and a public change record before adoption.
3. Treasury, ecosystem, community and validator releases should be traceable and governed by defined operational controls.
4. A burn mechanism is not enabled in v1 and requires separate approval before implementation.
5. Commercial launch remains gated by technical readiness, security validation, governance readiness and applicable legal/regulatory review.
6. Changes to production genesis, validator configuration, chain state, DNS, RPC, secrets or activation status require a separate operational review and are outside the scope of this document.

## External listing policy

CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, wallet registries, exchanges, explorers and other public services must receive supply and market information that matches verifiable public evidence. Do not describe the target circulating supply, hypothetical US$29 scenario, market pairs, price, volume, exchange relationships or liquidity as observed facts unless independently verifiable.

Public material may describe these figures as **KAM Tokenomics v1 / project economic baseline**. Network status must still be described independently and accurately. Until all launch-readiness gates are verified, the network must not be represented as a final public/commercial mainnet solely because tokenomics v1 has been adopted.
