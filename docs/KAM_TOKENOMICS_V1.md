# KAM Tokenomics v1

Status: **Proposed v1 — pending final approval**

KAM is the native asset of the KriptoAman network. This document establishes a transparent first-version economic framework for review before any commercial launch, public distribution, exchange activity, or promotional use.

## Core parameters

- Asset: KriptoAman
- Symbol: KAM
- Decimals: 18
- Genesis supply: 1,000,000,000 KAM
- Guaranteed return: No
- Guaranteed price: No
- Automatic daily tokenomics changes: No

## Proposed allocation

| Category | Allocation | KAM | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Ecosystem & Development | 35% | 350,000,000 | Protocol development, ecosystem applications, developer programs and long-term network growth |
| Treasury & Strategic Reserve | 20% | 200,000,000 | Infrastructure resilience and long-term strategic initiatives |
| Liquidity & Market Infrastructure | 15% | 150,000,000 | Planned liquidity and market infrastructure, subject to legal and launch requirements |
| Team & Contributors | 15% | 150,000,000 | Long-term contributor alignment with vesting |
| Community & Adoption | 10% | 100,000,000 | Education, community programs and ecosystem adoption |
| Strategic Partnerships | 5% | 50,000,000 | Qualified partnerships, integrations and expansion |

Total: **100% / 1,000,000,000 KAM**.

## Proposed vesting framework

- Team & Contributors: 12-month cliff, then linear vesting over 36 months.
- Strategic Partnerships: 6-month cliff, then linear vesting over 24 months.
- Ecosystem & Development: milestone- and governance-controlled release.
- Treasury & Strategic Reserve: governance-controlled release.
- Community & Adoption: program- and milestone-controlled release.
- Liquidity & Market Infrastructure: release only when launch-readiness and applicable compliance requirements are satisfied.

## Utility direction

KAM is intended to support the KriptoAman ecosystem. Proposed utility areas include network transaction fees, application and developer ecosystem participation, infrastructure services, ecosystem incentives, and future governance functions where technically and legally appropriate.

No utility in this document should be interpreted as a promise of financial return, price appreciation, guaranteed listing, or guaranteed liquidity.

## Governance safeguards

1. Supply, allocation, vesting, treasury, liquidity and burn parameters must not be changed automatically.
2. Material economic changes require explicit approval, versioned documentation and a public change record before adoption.
3. Treasury and ecosystem releases should be traceable and governed by defined operational controls.
4. A burn mechanism is not enabled in v1 and requires separate approval before implementation.
5. Commercial launch remains gated by technical readiness, security validation, governance readiness and applicable legal/regulatory review.

## Publication policy

Until this proposal is explicitly adopted, public campaign material should describe these figures as **proposed tokenomics**, not final tokenomics. Network status must also be described accurately and must not imply a public commercial mainnet before that state is independently verified.
