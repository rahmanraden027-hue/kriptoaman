# KriptoAman / KAM — Real Economy Activation Framework

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK

## Objective

Build measurable economic activity around KriptoAman and KAM without relying on artificial token price, fabricated liquidity, wash trading, or an unverified public token-sale shortcut.

## Economic engines

### 1. Enterprise service revenue
KriptoAman may earn normal company revenue from contracted technology services:

- market data/API access;
- dedicated KAM RPC capacity;
- managed node infrastructure;
- Web3 integration projects;
- security/network monitoring;
- technical architecture and readiness work.

Revenue is recognized only when supported by a real customer relationship, contract/order, invoice, payment evidence, and applicable tax/accounting records.

### 2. KAM network utility
KAM is positioned as the native network asset for legitimate on-chain utility such as gas and network interactions. Economic evidence should be measured through real transactions and real external users/applications, not treasury self-transactions designed to create volume.

### 3. Future DEX fee economy
DEX fee revenue is a later-stage engine. It remains gated by independent security review, quote-asset provenance, custody approval, treasury authorization, controlled canary testing, and applicable legal/regulatory review.

No public DEX revenue is recognized before the relevant gates are satisfied.

All prices are commercial starting points, not financial forecasts. Final scope, taxes, SLA, payment terms, and deliverables require a customer agreement.

## Evidence standard for "real economy"

KriptoAman should only claim measurable economic activity when at least one of the following exists:

- third-party customer invoice paid;
- third-party subscription paid;
- documented service contract with payment received;
- independently verifiable network fee paid by a non-treasury user for real usage;
- legitimate DEX fee earned from independent user activity after DEX release gates pass.

Treasury transfers between controlled wallets, self-swaps, circular transactions, internal token transfers, or indicative token-price scenarios do **not** qualify as external revenue or market demand.

## Commercial operating procedure

1. Receive inbound enterprise inquiry.
2. Identify company/customer and technical need.
3. Run basic counterparty/compliance screening appropriate to the engagement.
4. Define scope, deliverables, exclusions, price, tax treatment, SLA, data/privacy responsibilities, and payment terms.
5. Use a signed order/contract or equivalent written acceptance.
6. Issue invoice through PT Kripto Aman Indonesia using current synchronized corporate/tax data.
7. Receive payment into the authorized company account/payment rail.
8. Deliver service and record acceptance evidence.
9. Reconcile revenue, costs, and tax/accounting treatment.
10. Record anonymized commercial metrics without exposing customer confidential information.

## Treasury separation

Company operating revenue, KAM treasury assets, DEX quote assets, and LP positions must be tracked separately in internal accounting.

Do not use customer operational funds as DEX liquidity without explicit legal basis, contract authority, accounting treatment, and applicable regulatory compliance.

## Market-integrity rules

KriptoAman will not:

- create fictitious volume;
- self-trade to manufacture demand;
- fabricate a USDC/USDT/WETH-like asset and represent it as externally backed;
- publish an internal KAM scenario price as an observed market price;
- guarantee token returns;
- market internal treasury transfers as adoption;
- describe an internal security review as an independent audit.

## 30-day execution target

- publish Enterprise service catalogue after build/review;
- acquire first qualified pilot lead;
- convert at least one lead into a written paid service engagement where commercially feasible;
- measure RPC/network utilization by external users separately from internal monitoring;
- obtain at least one attributable external smart-contract review or documented independent reviewer engagement, including pro-bono/community pathways;
- preserve DEX production HOLD until its specific gates pass.

## Success metric

The first milestone is **not a KAM price target**.

The first milestone is a documented external payment or independently verifiable external network usage demonstrating that a third party derives enough utility from KriptoAman/KAM infrastructure to incur a real economic cost.

That evidence becomes the foundation for sustainable growth, treasury planning, future liquidity, and credible market access.
