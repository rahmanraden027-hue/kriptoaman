# KriptoAman / KAM — Revenue & Market-Integrity Guard

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: ACTIVE INTERNAL CONTROL

## Purpose

Separate ordinary technology-service revenue from regulated digital-asset trading activity and prevent economic-growth initiatives from being misrepresented as investment returns, token-sale authorization, exchange approval, or public DEX authorization.

## Allowed commercial focus before DEX release

KriptoAman may pursue ordinary technology engagements such as data/API services, RPC capacity, node infrastructure, monitoring, integration engineering, and technical documentation, subject to normal contract, tax, privacy, security, and company-law requirements.

## Activities requiring separate legal/regulatory confirmation

Do not activate or market the following merely because the technology exists:

- public token sale or solicitation to Indonesian retail users;
- acting as a digital financial asset trader/exchange/custodian without the required status;
- receiving or controlling customer trading funds without the required legal framework;
- public DEX promotion where the product may fall within regulated trading activity;
- derivatives, yield promises, guaranteed returns, or investment-like products;
- representing KAM as admitted to an OJK/Exchange asset list unless independently confirmed;
- representing Circle, an exchange, regulator, auditor, wallet provider, or data platform as having approved KAM unless explicit evidence exists.

## Current Indonesian regulatory reference

The operating team must re-check current OJK requirements immediately before any regulated public launch. As of this document date, the relevant framework includes POJK 27/2024, POJK 23/2025, and PADK OJK 3/2026 for Digital Financial Asset trading, including crypto assets.

This document is an internal operational control and is not a legal opinion.

## Marketing controls

Permitted language:

- infrastructure available;
- public RPC / explorer available when technically verified;
- service catalogue available;
- KAM is intended as native network utility;
- audit/review status stated exactly;
- indicative internal scenarios clearly labelled as scenarios.

Prohibited or gated language:

- guaranteed profit;
- guaranteed listing;
- risk-free;
- officially approved by OJK/Circle/exchange without evidence;
- independent audit completed when only internal review exists;
- KAM DEX live before release gates pass;
- USDC.e/native USDC available without verified provenance and integration.

## Revenue evidence

Company revenue must be supported by real counterparty evidence. Self-transfers, treasury movements, internal gas tests, or internal swaps do not count as customer revenue or external adoption.

## Liquidity control

Treasury-owned liquidity may only proceed after the applicable security, custody, quote-asset, treasury, and legal gates are complete. Liquidity must not be seeded to manufacture a target token price or market-cap narrative.

## Escalation

Any ambiguity about whether a planned feature constitutes regulated digital-asset trading, custody, solicitation, investment product, or derivatives activity => HOLD public activation and obtain qualified Indonesian legal/regulatory review or written regulator/exchange guidance.
