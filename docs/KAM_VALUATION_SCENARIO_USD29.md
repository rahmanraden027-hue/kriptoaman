# KAM Valuation Scenario — US$29 Reference Case

## Purpose
This document presents an indicative valuation scenario for KAM, the native asset of KriptoAman Mainnet. The US$29 figure is a scenario reference for strategic planning and economic analysis only.

It is not the current market price, not a guaranteed listing price, not an investment recommendation, and not a promise that KAM will trade at US$29.

## Network and Supply Assumptions
- Asset: KAM
- Network: KriptoAman Mainnet
- Chain ID: 22028 (`0x560c`)
- Decimals: 18
- Maximum supply: 1,000,000,000 KAM
- Target initial circulating supply: 50,000,000 KAM (5%)
- Scenario reference price: US$29 per KAM

## Scenario Valuation
If an independent public market were to establish a price of US$29 per KAM:

### Circulating Market Capitalization
50,000,000 KAM × US$29 = **US$1,450,000,000**

### Fully Diluted Valuation
1,000,000,000 KAM × US$29 = **US$29,000,000,000**

These values are mathematical scenario outputs and do not represent current market capitalization or current FDV.

## Interpretation
KriptoAman does not set a public market price through this document. Any future market price must arise from legitimate price discovery supported by actual buyers and sellers, real liquidity, network utility, and trading venues that independently determine market value.

Factors that may influence any future valuation include network adoption, KAM utility, transaction activity, wallet and exchange integrations, validator participation, ecosystem growth, liquidity quality, treasury management, transparency, security, and regulatory/compliance considerations.

No individual factor guarantees a specific market price.

## Supply Discipline
The proposed maximum supply is 1,000,000,000 KAM. The target initial circulating supply is 50,000,000 KAM (5%). Locked, vested, treasury-controlled, validator-incentive, ecosystem, team, partnership, or otherwise unavailable allocations must not be represented as circulating unless they are genuinely unlocked and transferable under the published supply methodology.
